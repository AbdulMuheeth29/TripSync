## Pre-Written Responses to Common Questions

**Q: Is this free?**
A: Yes! Free tier includes 3 trips with 8 members each and 10 AI generations per month. Plenty for most casual travelers. Pro tier ($9.99/mo) is for power users.

**Q: How does AI trip planning work?**
A: You enter your destination, dates, and preferences (budget, interests, travel style). Claude AI generates a complete day-by-day itinerary with activities, restaurants, and logistics. You can regenerate or manually edit anything.

**Q: Do my friends need accounts?**
A: Yes, but it's free and takes 30 seconds. This ensures everyone can collaborate, vote, and see trip updates.

**Q: Can I use this for solo travel?**
A: Absolutely! The AI trip planner works great for solo trips too. Just create a trip with yourself as the only member.

**Q: What if I don't like the AI itinerary?**
A: You can regenerate it with different preferences, manually edit/delete items, or create your itinerary from scratch. AI is a starting point, not a requirement.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. We never sell data. Trips are private by default. Only invited members can see your trip. Optional public sharing is read-only.

**Q: What happens if I go over the free limits?**
A: You can upgrade to Pro ($9.99/mo) or simply archive older trips. We never delete your data.
